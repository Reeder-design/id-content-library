How to use the Lab

The Learning Content Lab is a public library of reusable instructional-design work: templates, interactions, prompts, job aids, code, frameworks, experiments, and other building blocks.

Finding something useful

  • 1
    Search naturally. Try a topic, tool, format, skill, or interaction type.
  • 2
    Narrow it down. Filter by content type, tool, or tag when the library gets crowded.
  • 3
    Open an item. Each item page can include a live preview, usage notes, reusable source files, and downloads.

What the status means

StableReady to reuse, adapt, or share.
ExperimentalUseful work that is still being tested or refined.
ArchivedKept for reference, but not actively maintained or recommended.

Lab vs. portfolio

The portfolio is the curated highlight reel. This Lab is the broader workbench: reusable pieces, experiments, templates, and practical building blocks that may never need a full case study.
